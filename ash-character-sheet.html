The Unstable Ash

Who was I before the fire?

An amnesiac changeling warlock whose true identity, background and moral alignment are completely unknown, even to Ash. Wearing a nondescript traveler persona, Ash reacts to the party and the world as things happen, and discovers who they are through play.

How to play Ash

Default Drift
No fixed personality to defend. Let Ash lean toward whoever the party is and whatever the moment calls for, and notice what feels natural. Those instincts are clues too.
The persona
"Ash" is a humble traveler: forgettable on purpose, polite, light on details. A mask that hides an empty space.
Alignment
Unknown. Chaotic Neutral on paper, but each choice is a hint at who Ash really was.

Current goal

Move from town to town under the name Ash, surviving on stolen faces and quick wits, while searching for:

  • Any trace of the sister and the silver pendant.
  • The truth behind the missing memories and the night the village burned.

Backstory

I

The Awakening

Ash woke alone in the smoldering cinders of an utterly destroyed village, with no memory of their past, their real name, or that night. Covered in soot and blood, their changeling nature surfaced as an instinct to survive. In a pool of water, Ash saw it for the first time: a pale, featureless face.

II

The Sister

One image keeps returning: a girl's laughter, and a silver pendant. Her body was not among the dead. Was she taken? Did she escape? Or is she running from Ash?

III

The Whispering Grimoire

Lying in the ash beside them was a leather-bound Book of Shadows. It whispers in a distant, melodic, fey-like cadence. Through it, Ash casts Hex, Armor of Agathys and ritual magic from muscle memory, with no idea where they learned them.

IV

The Horrifying Doubt

Was Ash an innocent victim, saved by an unseen fey entity during the attack? Or did Ash's own suppressed magic erupt that night and cause the massacre?

      In a fight

      Curse the biggest threat with Hex, then cast Eldritch Blast every turn from far away. Your one spell slot comes back on a Short Rest, so spend it.

      1 Reaction
      One per round, even on other turns. For Ash, usually an Opportunity Attack with the dagger.
      Free interaction
      One small thing, like drawing your dagger or opening a door.

      One slot per turn. You can spend only one spell slot on your turn. Hex plus Eldritch Blast is fine (cantrips are free); Hex plus Armor of Agathys on the same turn is not.

      Ash's combat routine

      1. First turn: Bonus Action Hex on the most dangerous enemy you can see, then Action Eldritch Blast it. Your slot is now spent.
      2. Every turn after: Eldritch Blast. Each hit adds Hex's 1d6.
      3. Target drops? On a later turn, use your Bonus Action to move Hex to a new enemy. No slot needed while you keep concentrating.
      4. Enemy next to you? Ranged attacks have Disadvantage when an enemy is within 5 ft. Use Shocking Grasp so it can't make Opportunity Attacks, then walk away.
      5. Expecting melee? Cast Armor of Agathys before the fight instead of Hex.

      Hex lasts up to 1 hour. If you keep concentrating, it can carry into the next fight.

      Concentration

      • You can hold one Concentration spell at a time. Ash's are Hex and Guidance; starting one ends the other.
      • When you take damage, make a Constitution save (+2). The DC is 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher (max 30). Fail and the spell ends.
      • It also ends if you're Incapacitated or drop to 0 HP.
      • The sheet's Concentrating box tracks which spell is active and reminds you to save when you take damage.

      Your spell slot in a fight

      • One 1st-level slot at level 1. It returns on a Short or Long Rest.
      • Hex and Armor of Agathys cost the slot. Cantrips never do.
      • Spell save DC 13, spell attack +5. You need a free hand or a focus; the Book of Shadows works as your focus.
      • From level 2: Magical Cunning gives the slot back mid-day with a 1-minute rite, once per Long Rest. Good between fights.

      Easy mistakes

      • Casting Hex and Armor of Agathys on the same turn.
      • Forgetting Hex's 1d6 on every hit.
      • Blasting while an enemy stands next to you. That's Disadvantage; Shocking Grasp first.
      • Holding the slot "for later". It comes back on a Short Rest.
      • Casting Guidance in combat and losing Hex.

      At 0 hit points

      • You fall Unconscious and make a death save at the start of each turn: 10 or higher succeeds.
      • Three successes and you stabilize; three failures and you die. A natural 20 brings you back with 1 HP; a natural 1 counts as two failures.
      • Any healing wakes you up. Taking damage while down counts as a failure.
      • Concentration ends, so Hex is gone.

      Between fights

      This is where a changeling con artist shines: new faces, quick lies, quiet magic and a familiar that sees for you.

      Shapechanging

      • Changing your appearance and voice is an Action, at will, and lasts until you change again.
      • Change before you're seen. Shifting mid-conversation is obvious.
      • Your clothes and gear don't change, so carry a costume or two.
      • You can't copy a specific person you've never seen.
      • Log each face on the Story tab so you remember who has seen which one.

      Talking your way through

      • Deception (+5) keeps a disguise or a lie believable. Persuasion (+5) for honest asks, Intimidation (+5) for threats.
      • Insight (+3) tells you if someone is lying or suspicious of you.
      • Cast Guidance before an important check: +1d4. It needs Concentration, so drop Hex first.
      • In the Feywild, words and bargains matter. Listen for exact wording in any deal.

      Quiet magic

      • Minor Illusion: a voice calling from another room, a crate to hide behind. Physical contact or a careful look reveals it.
      • Mage Hand: lift keys, open doors and plant small items from 30 ft away.
      • Cantrips never use your slot, so use them freely.

      Rituals from your book

      • Rituals cost no slot but take 10 extra minutes. Plan them for quiet moments.
      • Comprehend Languages: read a letter or understand a conversation for an hour (Sylvan is common here).
      • Find Familiar: takes about 1 hour 10 minutes as a ritual and burns 10 GP of incense.
      • Casting a ritual interrupts a Short or Long Rest. Do it before or after.

      Your familiar

      • A spirit in a small animal form (owl, cat, spider, raven and so on). You choose the form and its type: Celestial, Fey or Fiend.
      • Within 100 ft you can talk to it telepathically, and as a Bonus Action see and hear through it until your next turn. Send it to scout.
      • It can deliver your touch spells, like Shocking Grasp, and can take the Help action.
      • If it drops to 0 HP it vanishes. Cast the ritual again to bring it back.

      Locks, forgery & sneaking

      • Thieves' Tools (+4) for locks and traps.
      • Forgery Kit (+4) for fake papers, seals and letters. Pairs well with a stolen face.
      • Stealth (+4) and Sleight of Hand (+4) for slipping by and lifting things.

      Rewriting your Book of Shadows

      • At the end of every Short or Long Rest, you can conjure a new Book of Shadows. The old one disappears.
      • When it appears, choose 3 cantrips and 2 level 1 Ritual spells from any class's list. They count as Warlock spells while the book is on you.
      • You can't pick spells you already have prepared, so not Eldritch Blast or Minor Illusion.
      • Lost the book? Rest and conjure a new one.

      Tune it for the day ahead. Heading into a city of humans? Friends works there. Exploring ruins? Light and Detect Magic. Expecting a fight? Vicious Mockery or Mind Sliver. Browse every option in the Reference tab under All cantrips and Ritual spells.

      Story note: your backstory's whispering grimoire is a special book. Ask your DM how re-conjuring fits the fiction, for example the book rewriting its own pages as you sleep.

      Short Rest 1 hour

      • Pact slot comes back. The big one for a Warlock.
      • Spend Hit Point Dice to heal: roll 1d8+2 per die. Ash has 1 die at level 1.
      • Conjure a new Book of Shadows with fresh picks, if you like.
      • Interrupted by rolling Initiative, taking damage, or casting any spell except a cantrip, including rituals.
      • Ask for one whenever your slot is spent. Most groups take one or two per day.

      On the sheet: Short rest restores the slot; then use Spend & roll under Hit Points.

      Long Rest 8 hours

      • Regain all HP, all Hit Point Dice and all pact slots.
      • Magical Cunning (level 2+) can be used again.
      • Remove 1 level of Exhaustion.
      • Conjure a new Book of Shadows with fresh picks, if you like.
      • At least 6 hours of sleep; up to 2 hours of light activity like talking, reading or keeping watch.
      • Interrupted by Initiative, damage, casting a non-cantrip spell, or an hour of walking or hard work. If at least 1 hour passed first, you still get Short Rest benefits.
      • Only one Long Rest per 24 hours.

      On the sheet: Long rest resets HP, hit die, slot, temp HP, concentration and death saves.

      When Ash levels up

      • Add HP: take the average of 5 plus your Constitution modifier (7), or roll 1d8 plus it. You also gain a Hit Point Die.
      • Each level, you may swap one Warlock cantrip, one prepared spell and one invocation. You can't swap out an invocation another one depends on.
      • Check the Warlock table in the Reference tab for new invocations, cantrips, prepared spells and slots.
      Lv 22 more invocations (Agonizing Blast is the standout), Magical Cunning, a second slot, a third prepared spell.
      Lv 3Choose your patron. Slots become 2nd level.
      Lv 4A feat or +2 to ability scores (Charisma to 18 is typical), plus a third Warlock cantrip.
      Lv 53rd-level slots, 2 more invocations, and Eldritch Blast fires 2 beams.
